About This Project

Why this site exists, and the instructional design behind it.

📚 Curriculum 📝 Diagnostic 🎯 Practice 🔥 Warm-Up ℹ️ About

I built JH Math for one of my tutoring students heading into Grade 5 — a full curriculum reference, a diagnostic test, targeted practice, and a warm-up system, all generated dynamically rather than handed over as static worksheets. It's still in active weekly use, not a one-off demo.

1

Diagnostic before practice

I didn't want to guess where the gaps were. The 20-question diagnostic scores every strand separately and links straight into targeted practice for whatever comes back weak — practice time goes where it's actually needed, not down a fixed worksheet order.

2

Real curriculum alignment

Every strand and expectation on the Curriculum page is pulled directly from the Ontario Mathematics Curriculum (2020), organized the way a teacher actually plans around it — not simplified into generic "math skills."

3

Practice that gets used again

Questions are generated fresh each time, not pulled from a fixed bank, and progress is saved on-device so a student can pick up where they left off. The goal was a tool a kid actually opens again next week, not one they click through once.

Part of a broader curriculum design practice

This project reflects the same approach I bring to instructional design generally: start from where the student actually is, build around real data, and design practice students will come back to.
